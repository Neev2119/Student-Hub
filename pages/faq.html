01 / Account

Getting started

Set up your student access and keep your details current.

How do I log in to StudentHub?

Open the Login page and enter the student email address and password you used during registration. Keep your details private and sign out on shared computers.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Use the password reset option on the Login page. If you cannot access your student email, contact the StudentHub team so they can verify your account.

Can I update my profile information?

For changes to your name, department, or student record, contact Student Services. Academic records are updated only after verification.

02 / Academics

Classes and progress

Keep an eye on the information that shapes your term.

Can I view my attendance online?

Yes. The Attendance page shows your current subject-wise record. If a recent class is missing, share the course and date with your instructor.

Where can I find my timetable and courses?

Use Courses to review your enrolled subjects and Timetable to plan your weekly classes. The navigation bar links to both pages.

03 / Assignments

Deadlines and submissions

Know where to look before the next deadline arrives.

Where can I see my assignments?

Visit Assignments from the top navigation. You will see the subject, task name, due date, and current submission status together.

What if an assignment shows the wrong status?

Confirm that you are viewing the correct subject and then contact your instructor with the assignment name and submission date so the record can be checked.
